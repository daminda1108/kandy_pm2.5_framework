## Structure of the thesis {#s-structure-thesis}

{{ref:ch-kandy-setting-record-stakes}} sets out why the problem is sharpest in a place like Kandy and what is at stake there.
{{ref:ch-prior-measurement-gap-leaves}} reviews what has actually been measured in the city over two decades and identifies
what none of those studies could establish. {{ref:ch-available-data-deliberate-constraint}} describes the data and computational
resources available for the work.

{{ref:ch-eight-approaches-did-work}} is an account of what was attempted and did not succeed. It is placed in the middle of
the thesis and not in an appendix because the pattern in those failures turned out to be the
most useful thing the project learned. {{ref:ch-model}} sets out the model that did work, {{ref:ch-validation-without-local-ground}}
describes how it was checked, and {{ref:ch-model-stops}} establishes where it stops and why the stopping
point is a property of the question rather than a deficiency of the method. {{ref:ch-measure-next}} sets out
what to measure next and the measured value on which that choice rests. {{ref:ch-reproducibility-machinery-catches-errors}} documents the software and the
reproducibility machinery.
