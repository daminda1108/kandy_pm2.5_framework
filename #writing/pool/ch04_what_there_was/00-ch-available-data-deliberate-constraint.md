# The available data, and a deliberate constraint {#ch-available-data-deliberate-constraint}

{{ref:ch-prior-measurement-gap-leaves}} established that Kandy has no continuous measurement record. {{This:ch-available-data-deliberate-constraint}} sets out what
it does have, what was obtained from elsewhere, and what was requested and never arrived. The
constraint that shapes everything after it is stated first, because it was a choice rather than
an accident.
