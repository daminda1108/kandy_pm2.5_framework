## Summary of established results {#s-summary-established-results}

The measurement establishes, on {{claim:v2.conf.n_cities}} cities registered before their data were
retrieved, that the value of each observation stream can be measured rather than argued, and it
confirms the registered verdicts as constructed: the first two stations used as a recalibration
help, four more add nothing to that recalibration, and a same-network background series read on the
day helps more. Those verdicts survive a richer sensorless rung, three other learners and full station
networks.

It establishes, in a re-analysis made after those results were known and therefore exploratory, why
the registered ordering came out as it did. A station whose reading reaches the estimate on the day
reduces daily city-mean error by more than half, and the same station used only to recalibrate a free
estimate reduces it by about a tenth. Used alike, a local pair and a background series are worth the
same, and more stations read on the day add a little, flattening after a handful. **What a station is
worth to a daily city mean depends on how its readings are used far more than on what kind of station
it is.** That is the evidence behind the measurement priorities for Kandy in the main text.

It does not establish any ordering of local stations against a background series as a property of the
observations, and the thesis does not state one. It does not establish anything specific to the deep
tropics: the earlier deep-tropical reversal was one draw and is retracted, and the confirmation cannot
detect a dependence on latitude.

**The scope of these statements is the panel, and the panel is not the world.** The confirmed
population is mainly temperate and mainly regulatory, and Kandy, deep-tropical and monitored by
low-cost sensors, lies outside its bulk. Every city in it publishes enough concurrent monitoring to be
scored, which selects for exactly the institutional capacity that the cities this method exists for do
not have. Where a result is quoted outside this thesis, the qualifier that belongs with it is across
the monitored cities that could be assembled, and not globally.

It does not establish that the model is accurate at Kandy, because that cannot be established with the
observations Kandy has. In particular it does not establish that the neighbourhood-scale field is
correct, and {{ref:ch-model-stops}} gives the measurement showing that it is not, together with the
reason no product built from the available covariates would be. What {{this:a-app-panel}} establishes
is what the model is entitled to claim, and what Kandy should measure next.
