## Approaches that would not help {#s-approaches-would-help}

Stated because these are the proposals most likely to be made.

**A larger model.** {{ref:ch-eight-approaches-did-work}} records several architectures of increasing capacity, none of
which recovered information that was not in the inputs.

A finer grid, for the reason given in {{ref:s-implications-radius-result}}.

More monitors in the same city, sited as networks conventionally site them, for the reason
given in {{ref:s-measurement-priority-ordering}}. Siting them deliberately across land-use contrast instead is a different
proposition, and the measured redundancy of additional monitors says nothing about it; what does
bear on it is {{ref:s-six-negative-results-their}}, which finds no paired advantage for deliberate siting on the panel's
dense networks.

More cities in the panel, unless they are chosen to break the class-band association, which
{{ref:ch-kandy-setting-record-stakes}} established cannot be done with the cities that currently publish data.
