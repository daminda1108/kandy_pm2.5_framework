## The sentence to carry away {#s-sentence-carry-away}

Two statements should survive any summary of this work, and they pull in opposite directions.

**The method is transferable, and what it measured is that a station is worth its daily
reading.** A city with no monitors can be told what its next observation is worth, in units of
predictive error, on evidence registered and confirmed on {{claim:v2.conf.n_cities}} fresh cities
with the target withheld from every fit, and robust to the baseline, the learner and the station
cap. Read on the same footing, two stations of any kind reduce the error of a daily city mean by
{{claim:v2.review.registered_loco.reco.gL2s_rmse.median}} per cent; used only as a calibration
they recover a small fraction of that.

The absolute concentration scale at Kandy is not independently validated. The temporal anchor
is calibrated against the city's own two low-cost sensors, so the external records of {{ref:s-checks-kandy-carry-weight}}
test the modelled lift above an already anchored mean, not the level itself. Three of the
four independent point records sit below the model, and the one that agrees carries an undocumented
instrument. Nothing in this thesis resolves that, and a reference-grade measurement at Kandy is
the only thing that would. Until one exists, the field should be read as a well-founded relative
and spatial construction on a level that remains open.
