# Abstract {.front}

::: {custom-style="Abstract Text"}
Kandy, a valley city in Sri Lanka's central highlands with about 99,000 residents and nearly 389,000 weekday commuters, has one regulatory monitor for fine particulate matter (PM2.5) and two low-cost sensors. This study builds a constrained hourly one-kilometre reconstruction of Kandy's PM2.5 for 2019 to 2023 from free satellite, reanalysis and geographic data, and tests how far each part holds. The field is a uniform regional background plus a local increment carrying all spatial structure; the city-wide annual mean equals the temporal estimate, with small hourly departures. The modelled annual means, {{claim:kandy.mean_min}} to {{claim:kandy.mean_max}} micrograms per cubic metre, exceed the World Health Organization guideline every year, and remain at least {{claim:ceabeta.who_lo}} times it across the level's calibration interval. The seasonal cycle carries to all {{claim:scorecard.cities}} monitored cities scored on withheld stations, no better than their two anchor stations' climatology, and matches a national Kandy record across calendar months (correlation {{claim:nbrod.climatology_r}}) but not day to day ({{claim:nbrod.anomaly_daily_r}}). Across {{claim:v2.km.cities_scored}} cities, the deployed temporal model reduces daily error by {{claim:v2.km.reco.unio.gK2_rmse.median}} per cent against a generic sensorless estimate when evaluated retrospectively over periods its two anchor stations observed; prospectively the reduction is {{claim:v2.km.pros.unio.gK2_rmse.median}} per cent ({{claim:v2.km.pros.unio.gK2_rmse.lo}} to {{claim:v2.km.pros.unio.gK2_rmse.hi}}), not an established improvement. Against the city-centre regulatory station the field follows the day-to-day sequence (daily correlation {{claim:cea.del.all.r}}); its satellite-derived level read {{claim:cea.sat.all.bias_pct}} per cent high; re-anchored on 2019 to 2021, it is within {{claim:ceasplit.bias_re}} per cent on held-out days, but {{claim:cea.del.2022.bias_pct}} per cent in 2022 and +{{claim:cea.del.2023.bias_pct}} per cent in 2023, a provisional one-station correction. The spatial pattern is a hypothesis, not a validated neighbourhood map. The local increment is {{claim:partition.f}} of the modelled total ({{claim:v2.f.cap_min}} to {{claim:v2.f.cap_max}} across specifications), not a source apportionment; at the city centre an urban-increment lower bound is {{claim:lfs.e1min.f}} and a combustion-tracer estimate (not an upper bound) {{claim:lfs.e2.co_p5.f}}; the model matches the latter but may be high. Across cities, two stations read daily cut error by {{claim:v2.review.k.day2.median}} per cent, against {{claim:v2.review.k.cal2.median}} when they only calibrate the estimate. For Kandy this favours daily-read stations and a network built to test, not fit, the field.
