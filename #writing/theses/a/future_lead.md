## Future work {#a-future}

The limits identified in {{ref:a-results}} each point to a specific piece of further work. The
measurements that would settle the most come first: continuously reporting stations whose
readings enter the estimate daily, among them a reference-grade instrument that would also settle
the level, followed by the construction step most worth revisiting, the improvements available to
the temporal model, including a humidity correction checked against a co-located reference, and
the proposals that the evidence says would not help.
