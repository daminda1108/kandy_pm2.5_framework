Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) is the air pollutant with the largest estimated burden of disease
worldwide [@GBD2021Risk], and the regions where it is least monitored are frequently those where
concentrations are highest [@Martin2019; @vanDonkelaar2021]. Kandy is one such city.
{{This:a-intro}} reviews why surface PM2.5 is difficult to estimate where it is not monitored,
describes the study area and its measurement record, reviews previous work there, and states the
aim, objectives and scope of the study.
