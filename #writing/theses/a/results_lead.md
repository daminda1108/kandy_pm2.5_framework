The results are presented in the order of the objectives. The field itself comes first, because
every later section asks a question about it: what it implies for exposure, whether independent
records at Kandy agree with it, whether the construction works in monitored cities of the same
kind, how the deployed model compares with what is achievable at Kandy's information budget,
whether its uncertainty intervals mean what they say, the scale at which its spatial pattern stops
being supported, and what the measured value of each kind of observation implies for what Kandy
should measure next. The discussion that closes the chapter draws those answers together and states how
a design flaw found in one registered comparison changes the measurement priorities.
