The results are presented in the order of the objectives. The field itself comes first, because
every later section asks a question about it: whether independent records at Kandy agree with it,
whether its uncertainty intervals mean what they say, what it implies for exposure, whether the
construction transfers between cities, the scale at which its spatial pattern stops being
supported, and what the measured value of each kind of observation implies for what Kandy should
measure next. The discussion that closes the chapter draws those answers together and states how
a design flaw found in one registered comparison changes the measurement priorities.
