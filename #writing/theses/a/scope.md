## Scope and structure {#a-scope}

The claims of this study are narrower than the problem it addresses, and are stated explicitly
here.

It does not claim a validated neighbourhood-scale map of Kandy. {{ref:ch-model-stops}} shows that such a
map cannot be supported at the resolution used, explains why the limit is one of definition
rather than of data, and shows that a handful of monitoring stations would not supply one either.
It does not claim that the model represents atmospheric chemistry, because it contains none, and
the share of concentration it assigns to the local increment is a bound set by a constraint, not
a source apportionment. It does not claim that the absolute concentration at Kandy is
independently validated, because the one external record that agrees with the field and the three
that sit below it have not been reconciled. And it does not claim that the construction has been
validated at Kandy itself. What is validated is a procedure, tested on monitored valley and basin
cities by withholding their own observations and applied to Kandy by analogy; Kandy is by
construction the kind of city that validation set excludes, so the transfer is an argument from
resemblance.

The measurement priorities are bounded in the same way. They rest on a cross-city measurement
whose registered panel is mostly temperate and mostly served by regulatory monitors, whereas Kandy
is tropical and has only low-cost sensors. They are stated for a daily city-mean estimate, and
they are stated as the evidence supports them after a design flaw in one registered comparison
was found and corrected ({{ref:a-discussion}}).

{{ref:a-methods}} describes the data, the decomposition and how a field can be checked where no
local ground truth exists. {{ref:a-results}} presents the reconstructed field, the checks it
passes and fails, the scale at which it stops, and what the measurement of information value
implies for what Kandy should measure next. {{ref:a-conclusions}} answers the objectives and sets out
the further work the results call for.
The appendices carry the supporting work in full: the measurement of information value across the
panel of monitored cities, the negative results on spatial structure, the approaches that did not
succeed, the chemical check on the decomposition, and the software that regenerates every number
in this document from its source.
