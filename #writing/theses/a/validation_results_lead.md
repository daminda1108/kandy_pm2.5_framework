## Validation {#a-validation}

The three lines of evidence set out in {{ref:ch-validation-without-local-ground}} are reported in
turn: the independent records at Kandy, the complete model in ten analogue cities, and the
deployed model's temporal anchor on the ladder of information. The section closes with the
calibration of the delivered interval, which draws on all three, and with the sensitivity of the
delivered series to the humidity correction. {{tbl:T3_validation_summary}} gathers the results by
evaluation condition, so that agreement on one timescale is not read as accuracy on another.
