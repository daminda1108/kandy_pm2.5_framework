## Objectives {#b-aims}

The aim set out in {{ref:s-aim-approach}} is pursued through five objectives.

1. To formulate a decomposition of fine particulate concentration with declared information
   budgets, in which removing an observation stream returns the simpler tier exactly
   ({{ref:b-methods}}).
2. To measure the marginal predictive value of each observation stream across a panel of
   monitored valley and basin cities, by withholding each city's own observations
   ({{ref:s-marginal-predictive-value-each}}).
3. To test whether that ordering survives the dependence between cities, the choice of estimator
   and scoring loss, a covariate trained on the same monitors, and the confounds of the panel
   ({{ref:s-recommendation-inverts-tropics}} to {{ref:s-three-confounds-pooled-numbers}}).
4. To establish, with detection limits fixed in advance, how much within-city spatial information
   the freely available covariates carry ({{ref:ch-model-stops}}).
5. To apply the measurement to Kandy, a city with no reference monitor, and derive the order in
   which its observations should be obtained ({{ref:ch-measure-next}}).
