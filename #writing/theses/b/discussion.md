## Discussion {#b-discussion}

**What the measurement establishes.** The value of an observation stream can be measured rather
than argued, and the ordering it produces is not the one intuition supplies. Freely available
geography is worth about as much as a first local instrument. A second local station is worth
almost nothing for a daily city mean, and that is the most estimator-robust result of the study,
holding under every learner and every loss tried. A background series drawn from outside the urban
core is the largest single gain pooled across the panel, and an independent donor network
recovers {{claim:donor.gain_reproduced_pct}} per cent of that gain, so the step carries regional
information and is not merely more of the same network.

**Where it holds, and where it does not.** Every statement above is conditional, and the
conditions belong inside the sentence when it is quoted. The panel is the set of monitored valley
and basin cities that publish enough data to be scored, which is neither a sample of the world's
cities nor of the cities this method exists for. The cities are not independent:
{{claim:clust.largest_n}} of them share one national network, and resampling whole networks widens
every interval without overturning a conclusion. The ordering reverses between latitude bands, and
the band is a label rather than a mechanism, because instrument class and several other properties
travel with it. The ordering reverses again with the scoring loss: the local advantage in the deep
tropics becomes a background advantage of {{claim:loss.inv.exceedance}} points for exceedance
detection. And every gain is a marginal gain at a fixed position in a fixed order of adding
streams, measured by an estimator that shrinks back towards the tier below, so a step near zero
means that no improvement was attainable, not that the stream carries no information.

**Two methodological results travel beyond air quality.** A covariate trained on the same
monitors that are later withheld does not inflate its own step; it deflates the step above it,
which is why a test that looks for excess skill inside the contaminated stream finds none and the
contamination goes unreported. On this panel it was enough to make the deep-tropical inversion
indistinguishable from zero. And a difference of medians across a panel is not an effect: on four
occasions in this work the unpaired comparison disagreed with the comparison paired within city,
twice in sign, and each time the unpaired reading was the one the analyst would have preferred to
report. Both lessons were caught by registering the
analysis before running it, which is the practice {{ref:s-pre-registration-working-practice}} describes.

**What it means for a city without monitors.** A city like Kandy cannot validate a model of its
air, but it can be told, in units of predictive error and on evidence from other cities, what its
next observation is worth and what that ordering depends on. That is a narrower product than a
validated map. It is also one that is available now, before any instrument is bought, and it
names the instrument whose purchase would change the most.
