# Abstract {.front}

::: {custom-style="Abstract Text"}
Most of the world's population breathes air that no instrument measures, and the shortage is worst where concentrations are highest. Models fill the gap, but they are tested where monitors are dense and used where monitors are absent, so the transfer that matters most is the one that cannot be scored. This study measures instead what each further observation is worth. Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) is decomposed into a regional background and a local increment whose city-wide average is fixed, and each tier of the model declares which observation streams it may use; removing a stream returns the simpler tier exactly, so the difference between two tiers measures information rather than a change of model. Withholding observations from {{claim:frame.cities}} monitored valley and basin cities in {{claim:frame.countries}} countries shows that freely available geography is worth {{claim:step.geography}} per cent in daily error, comparable to a first local instrument; one local station buys {{claim:stn.one_gain}} per cent and a second adds {{claim:stn.second_adds}} points; and a regional background series is the largest single gain, at {{claim:step.bud2_bud3}} per cent. The ordering reverses in the deep tropics, where a local observation is worth {{claim:maiac.deep_tropical_first2}} per cent against {{claim:maiac.deep_tropical_background}} for the background, and it reverses again when scored on exceedance days rather than average ones. A satellite product trained on the same monitors, used as a covariate, makes that tropical inversion indistinguishable from zero by deflating the step above it rather than inflating its own. Within-city spatial pattern is a measured limit: across {{claim:tour.cities}} cities no model family beats the best single free raster by more than the registered detection limit of {{claim:phase1.min_detectable}} in rank correlation. Applied to Kandy, Sri Lanka, a city with two low-cost sensors and no reference monitor, the measurement ranks a local observation first for daily city-mean accuracy, best made reference grade because that also settles the absolute level, and a regional background series first for detecting episodes.
:::
