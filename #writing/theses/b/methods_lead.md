A measurement of what an observation is worth needs three things, and they are described in turn.
The first is a panel of cities where the observation exists and can be withheld, together with
the freely available data that every city has. The second is a model in which withholding is exact,
so that the difference between two tiers is the effect of the data and not of a changed model. The
third is a protocol that fixes, before anything is scored, what would count as evidence.
