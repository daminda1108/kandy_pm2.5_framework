## Structure {#b-structure}

{{ref:b-methods}} describes the data, the panel of borrowed cities, the decomposition with its
information budget and guarantees, and the protocol under which observations are withheld and
predictions registered in advance. {{ref:b-results}} reports the measured value of each observation
stream and every check made on it, the limits of within-city spatial information, and what the
measurement implies for Kandy. {{ref:b-conclusions}} answers the objectives and sets out further work.
The appendices carry the construction of the Kandy field and its local checks, the approaches that
did not succeed, the transfer between cities, the chemical check on the decomposition, and the
software that regenerates every number in this document from its source.
