# Numbers in the manuscript that the claims gate does not protect

`build_claims.py` emits 224 claims and the manuscript resolves 183 of them. Everything else that
looks like a number in the text falls into one of three groups. This file exists because the
third group is the dangerous one and it should be visible rather than implicit.

Last audited 2026-09-04, against the 18-figure build.

🟢 **Cleared 2026-09-04.** The Kandy–Colombo donor figures are now generated by
`scripts/colombo_donor_test.py` and tokenised. Re-running the test reproduced the
correlation exactly (0.604) and corrected two of the three quoted numbers: the
separation is 93.9 km measured city-centre, and the "distance-matched benchmark of
0.923" was never a median — it is the single nearest pair's value (0.928). The
benchmark is 0.846 pooled, 0.822 distance-matched. "Weakest of all 20" was also
wrong: one pair at 285 km scores lower. Kandy–Colombo IS the weakest at comparable
separation, which is the defensible form and the one now in §7.

🟢 **Also cleared 2026-09-04.** The NBRO pixel lift is generated by
`scripts/nbro_pixel_check.py` and tokenised, along with the model values in §6.5's
table. Everything reproduced exactly. One imprecision corrected: 15.6% is the 2021
lift and the paper quoted it as though it were the property — 2022 is 17.9%, and
both years are now given. The observed values stay cited to Nirmani et al.

## 2. Structural constants — not measurements

Configuration and definitions. Wrong only if the model changes, in which case the code changes
with them.

15 × 15 km domain · 1 km reporting resolution · the nominal 90 per cent interval · the 48-hour
episode window · 2019–2026 coverage · 80 per cent power as the design threshold · 1.0 as the
exponent for pure inverse-BLH dilution.

`subgrid.production_res_m` (238 m) is in this group but IS tokenised, because §5 compares it
against a second resolution and the two must be quoted from one place.

**Rule for this file.** A number moves out of group 3 only by acquiring a generating script or a
citation. It does not move by being checked once by hand — that is how all of these got here.
