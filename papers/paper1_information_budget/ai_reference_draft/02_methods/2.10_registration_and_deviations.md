### 2.10.3 The registry itself was audited

Before this paper, the project's own register of predictions undercounted: it omitted one
registration's five-prediction block and three untestable predictions, omitted two priors of
another, and counted one registration's gate passes rather than its predictions (38 predictions
recorded against 48 registered). It was corrected against every registration document and the
analysis ledger before any count in this paper was written [F.115].
