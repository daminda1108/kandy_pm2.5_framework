## 3.7 No free predictor, learner or siting design beats a single built-up covariate

Every test in this section uses the frame, estimand and benchmark of Section 2.8. The benchmark is the
fraction of built-up land within 2.4 km, used as it is. It has a median within-city rank correlation
of 0.301 on 47 cities and 636 stations. Table 3.5 gives each candidate's paired advantage over it.

**Table 3.5.** Paired within-city advantage over the built-up benchmark in rank correlation (median
[95 % bootstrap over cities]), cities won, and the smallest effect the test could detect (δ, 80 %
power). Registered tests are marked with their OSF identifier.

No candidate beats the benchmark by more than its test could detect. We read this as a bound: no
admissible method we tried improves the within-city ranking by more than about 0.1 to 0.2 in rank
correlation. It does not show that no such method exists. One quantity sits closest to a signal. The
embeddings' partial correlation after the benchmark is removed (E3) missed the registered bar by
0.007, which is roughly three times an earlier, underpowered estimate. It is the natural target for a
larger panel.

The inadmissible oracle arm, in which kriging, geographically weighted regression and
inverse-distance weighting see the target city's own stations, did worse than the admissible
benchmark: median ρ of 0.048, 0.073 and 0.190. Interpolating a city's own sparse network is a poorer
guide to its within-city ranking than a land-cover fraction from outside it.

Siting does not rescue the ranking either. Fitting on half of each city's stations chosen to span the
covariates, against half chosen by proximity to major roads, gave a paired difference of −0.044
[−0.095, +0.118]; deliberate siting won in 19 of 43 cities. The gap between published land-use
regression skill (R² 0.43 to 0.83) and the ρ of about 0.3 here is therefore not explained by how
regulatory networks are sited. [Author: the most likely explanation is that published campaigns sample
deliberate land-use contrasts at a few dozen sites within one city, while our estimand transfers across
cities with no local fitting; state this as a reading, not a result.]
