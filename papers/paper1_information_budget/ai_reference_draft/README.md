# AI reference draft — Paper 1 (NOT FOR SUBMISSION)

**What this folder is.** A complete reference draft of Paper 1 written by Claude (AI), exactly as
the final paper should read, so the author can learn the argument, the connecting logic, the
methods, the mathematics and the statistics. **The author rewrites every sentence himself** before
anything goes to supervisors or a journal. Copernicus journals (ACP, AMT, GMD) forbid
AI-generated text and interpretations; IOP, Elsevier and Nature require disclosure. A close
line-by-line paraphrase of this draft may still count as AI-generated, so the recommended method
is: read a section, close it, write your own version from your understanding, then compare.

**Title (confirmed 2026-09-25):**
*Declared information budgets: what an air-quality observation is worth to a city without monitors*

**Target:** Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics (research article; APC waived for Sri Lanka-based
authors); backup Environmental Research Letters (would need cutting to a 4,000-word letter).

## Rules this draft follows
- Every number carries a traceability marker such as `[F.113]` (ledger entry in
  `kandy_pm25/docs/model_reference/F_epistemic_ledger.md`) or `[claim: key]` (`claims.json`).
  Markers are removed or turned into citations before submission.
- Every analysis is labelled **registered** (with its OSF id) or **exploratory (post hoc)**.
- Nulls are stated as intervals against a detection limit ("undetectable at δ"), never as
  "no effect".
- Citations are pandoc keys `[@key]` in `../references/references.bib` (all verified 2026-10-05); `[TODO: ...]` marks work still to
  be done; `[PENDING: spatial curve]` marks text that waits for the registered spatial learning
  curve, whose results do not yet exist and are not anticipated anywhere in this draft.
- House style from `papers/WORKFLOW.md`: explain each figure before it appears; captions stand
  alone; no em dashes; subject close to verb; new information at sentence end.
